## 2. Factory Assembly & Field Commissioning Labor

Assembly uses a standardized assembly line producing 4 units per day, requiring **120 human labor hours per container**.

### Factory Floor Crew (35-Person Assembly Line)
* **4 Structural Welders:** Corten steel modification, canopy rails.
* **6 Electricians:** Harness routing, 48V bus, control panel wiring.
* **6 HVAC/Plumbing Specialists:** Inverter AC, closed-loop manifolds, UV loop.
* **10 Mechanical Assembly Techs:** Grow rack sliding, insulation installation.
* **4 Software/QC Testers:** BMS flash, sensor calibration, Starlink ping.
* **5 Logistics Operators:** Material handling, loading finished units.

### Labor Costing Summary
* **Factory Labor:** 120 hours x $45/hour (blended rate + overhead) = **$5,400 per unit**
* **Field Installation Labor:** 96 field hours x $30/hour = **$2,880 per unit**
* **Total Human Labor Cost:** **$8,280 per unit**
