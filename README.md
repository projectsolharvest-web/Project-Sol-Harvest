## 📌 Executive Overview

**Project Sol-Harvest** treats global food insecurity as a localized energy, hardware, and water-efficiency problem. The **AGHub** is an autonomous, off-grid 40ft ISO containerized indoor farming appliance capable of continuous crop production without municipal power or water tie-ins.

### Key Architectural Pillars
* **First-Principles Economics:** Capital Expenditure (CapEx) asset deployment financed via an un-dwindling $2.0B Purpose Trust model rather than infinite operational aid budgets.
* **Open-Source Infrastructure:** Complete release of CAD schematics, mechanical drawings, single-line electrical diagrams (SLDs), and edge telemetry under the **MIT License**.
* **Zero-Trust Telemetry & Verification:** Hardware-level sensor verification (load cells, multi-spectral vision AI, flowmeters) streamed via Starlink to an immutable, publicly auditable dashboard.
* **N+1 Scalable Microgrids:** Modular interconnect capabilities allowing individual AGHubs to link into high-capacity regional production complexes sharing power, water, and compute.
